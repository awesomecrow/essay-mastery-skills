# Essay Mastery Quality & Diagnostic Rubric

| Level | Ideal Standard (Grade A) | Minor Defect (Grade B/C) | Fatal Failure (Grade F) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Level 1 (Word)** | Zero clutter phrases; nominalizations < 5%; exact, unpretentious diction. | 1–3 clutter phrases; occasional sluggish nominalization. | Dense thesaurus camouflage; pervasive nominalization obscuring agency. |
| **Level 2 (Sentence)** | Sentences between 12–25 words; variation coefficient $\ge 0.35$; passive voice < 15%. | Monotone uniform sentence lengths; passive voice 15–25%. | Sentences > 40 words with runaway clauses; passive voice > 30%. |
| **Level 3 (Paragraph)** | 100–150 words per paragraph; exactly 1 clear thesis asserted in topic sentence. | Paragraphs 160–200 words or occasional stub < 40 words. | Runaway mega-paragraphs > 250 words; disjointed listicle stubs. |
| **Level 4 (Progression)** | Explicit logical stepping stones; swapping paragraph order breaks coherence. | Weak transition connective markers between sections. | Modular disjointed paragraphs whose sequence is interchangeable. |
| **Level 5 (The Whole)** | Answers topic question rigorously; final words within $\pm 5\%$ of target. | Deviates $\pm 15\%$ from word count target; weak thesis closure. | Evades core topic question; rambling unfinished discourse. |
| **Level 6 (The Reader)** | Anticipates and steel-mans strongest counterargument directly. | Token counterargument mentioned without thorough resolution. | Completely one-sided polemic with zero counterargument anticipation. |
| **Level 7 (Context)** | Consistent parenthetical citations (APA/MLA/Chicago) and bibliography. | Missing year or page numbers in parenthetical references. | Unsubstantiated factual assertions and unattributed quotations. |
