# Citation Integrity & Formatting Standards

Scholarly essays must situate their propositions in credible intellectual traditions. Below are conventions for major academic styles.

---

## 1. APA Style (7th Edition)
* **In-Text Parenthetical:** `(Pinker, 2014)` or `(Kahneman & Tversky, 1979, p. 265)`
* **In-Text Narrative:** Pinker (2014) argued that clarity stems from the elimination of the curse of knowledge.
* **Reference Entry:**
  ```text
  Pinker, S. (2014). The Sense of Style: The Thinking Person's Guide to Writing in the 21st Century. Penguin Books.
  ```

---

## 2. Chicago Notes & Bibliography (17th Edition)
* **Footnote Marker:** Sentence terminal superscript number.¹
* **Bibliography Entry:**
  ```text
  Peterson, Jordan B. Maps of Meaning: The Architecture of Belief. New York: Routledge, 1999.
  ```

---

## 3. MLA Style (9th Edition)
* **In-Text Parenthetical:** `(Orwell 12)`
* **Works Cited Entry:**
  ```text
  Orwell, George. "Politics and the English Language." Horizon, vol. 13, no. 76, 1946, pp. 252–265.
  ```
