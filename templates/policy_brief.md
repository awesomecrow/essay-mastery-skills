# Template: Policy & Strategic Brief Essay

> **Target Standard:** High-density, actionable decision paper, ~1,000–1,500 words.  
> **Philosophy:** Frame complex systemic challenges through explicit trade-offs and pragmatic implementation.

---

## 1. Executive Problem Definition
What immediate failure or strategic impasse necessitates structural intervention?

## 2. Root Cause Diagnostic
Distinguish between surface symptoms and foundational systemic drivers.

## 3. Analysis of Policy Alternatives & Trade-offs
- Option A: Status quo / incremental reform (Why it is insufficient).
- Option B: Radical restructuring (Associated risks and friction).
- Option C: Targeted strategic intervention (The recommended course).

## 4. Anticipated Resistance & Stakeholder Politics
Who loses power or resources under this proposal, and how is that friction neutralized?

## 5. Actionable Implementation Roadmap
Phased execution steps, accountability metrics, and measurable key performance indicators.
